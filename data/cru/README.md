The CRU TS4.09 data are monthly (0.5 x 0.5 degree) gridded fields based on monthly mean observational data calculated from daily (or finer) data (all CRU TS output files are actual values - NOT anomalies.)

Data in the raw directory are downloaded in (zipped, .gz) NetCDF (.nc) format from:
University of East Anglia Climatic Research Unit; Harris, I.C.; Jones, P.D.; Osborn, T. (2025): CRU TS4.09: Climatic Research Unit (CRU) Time-Series (TS) version 4.09 of high-resolution gridded data of month-by-month variation in climate (Jan. 1901- Dec. 2024). NERC EDS Centre for Environmental Data Analysis, date of citation. https://catalogue.ceda.ac.uk/uuid/9cf07e92afaa405da4f40b6733f362d3

Data in the clean directory are re-extracted from CRU, and all variables are merged in .csv format, with means extracted from polygons at the adm1 level to match the available case data.
